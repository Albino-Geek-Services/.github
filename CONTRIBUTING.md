# Contributing

Public product repositories will appear under this organization when released. Until then there is little to contribute against.

## When public repos exist

1. Open an issue before large changes.
2. Fork, branch, and open a pull request against `main`.
3. Sign off every commit (`git commit -s`) — this org requires the Developer Certificate of Origin.
4. Keep PRs focused; include a short “why” in the description.
5. Do not open PRs that only change formatting or docs fluff.

## What we are not looking for

- Drive-by dependency bumps without a clear need.
- Marketing PRs or “add me to README” noise.
- Requests to open private product source.
