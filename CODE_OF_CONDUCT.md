# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) (v2.1) in spirit: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion technical.

## Expectations

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- No spam, phishing, or abuse of issue/PR channels.

## Scope

Applies to public Albino Geek Services Ltd. GitHub spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) under this organization.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the operator via [albinogeek.com](https://albinogeek.com/). Maintainers may warn, hide, or ban as needed. Decisions are final for public community spaces.

Full text of the Contributor Covenant: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
